## How to sign

There is no form to fill in and nothing to e-mail. You sign this Agreement as
part of your pull request:

1. Open a pull request.
2. Add a comment on the pull request with exactly this text:

   > I have read the CLA document and hereby sign it.

3. The maintainer records your signature — your GitHub handle, the date of
   signing, and the pull-request number — in
   [`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](../CONTRIBUTORS.md) when your pull request is merged.

You only need to sign once; the recorded signature covers your future
Contributions as well.
